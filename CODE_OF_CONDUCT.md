# Code of conduct

search-hole adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Read it there. In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and leave no room for harassment of any kind.

It applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer, Basel Issmail, at the email address in the `author` field of [`packages/search-hole/package.json`](packages/search-hole/package.json). Mention "search-hole conduct" in the subject. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. If the report concerns the maintainer, say so, and it will be handled with a second person from outside the project.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently. The enforcement guidelines are those of the Contributor Covenant 2.1: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
